HOW IT WORKS

Refresh rate ≠ FPS

A display refresh rate is the panel's refresh frequency. Swap interval can make an app present a frame less often, creating a lower effective frame rate while the display itself runs at a higher supported refresh rate.

Effective FPS = Refresh Rate ÷ Swap Interval

Example: 90 Hz ÷ 2 = 45 FPS. These are frame-pacing recipes, not claims that the Quest panel has a native 45 Hz mode.
